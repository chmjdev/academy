# Plan — ANC CC Ops Academy

Roadmap and operational evolution for the digital training academy.

## Phase 1: Core Command Training (Current)
- Complete self-paced interactive delivery of all 15 operational modules.
- Video walkthroughs for core command desks: Orientation, Daily Ops, Rapid Rebuttal, WeVote Chatbot, 07:00 Intel, FB Ads, OSINT, and DCIP.
- Interactive quiz verification and knowledge retention checks.
- 10 sponsored student seats management.

## How-to guide library (2026-10-12)
- Step-by-step guides for every Govtech system at `/guides` (see `status.md`).
- Next: screenshots per guide; a refresh pass whenever a system's UI labels change; a branding decision on whether the multi-system library stays under the ANC CC Ops Academy name.

## Phase 2: Live Desk Simulation
- Simulated scenario drill engine where students practice logging breaking situations and running 2-hour SLA response countdowns.
- Synthetic draft review gate drills with simulated legal and compliance rejections.

## Phase 3: Field Communicator Accreditation
- Automated digital certificate generation with verifiable SHA-256 provenance hashes.
- Integration with provincial and regional command rosters.
